# Exact validation protocol, version 1

All instances and comparisons below are fixed before the full run. Run one process with single-thread BLAS. There are no sampled configurations in the product or Gibbs experiments. The independent-coefficient controls use Python's seeded MT19937. The serialized inputs give vertex and monomial indices starting at zero. The first index is the most significant bit of a lexicographically ordered assignment.

## Product lifts

Use the integer objectives f1–f4 (equations 46–49) and the mixed signed common-prefix control in inputs/instances.json. For each term with at least three variables, multiply the first two sorted indices, then append consecutive indices until two factors remain for the quadratic readout. The supplied term order determines gate numbering. Reuse a prefix across terms for the shared scheme and allocate a fresh chain per term for the separate scheme. Each readout term charges its positive and negative coefficient parts once per distinct ancestor. Enumerate every original and auxiliary assignment on both schemes (at most 7 original and 14 auxiliary bits). Verify each fiber minimum, the equality on its consistent extension, uniqueness for positive margin, and the violation-pattern lower bound at every point using integer arithmetic.

Use a common margin of one in three comparisons: directional A=P+1, B=N+1; symmetric local A=B=max(P,N)+1; symmetric global A=B=1+sum of absolute readout coefficients over terms with gate ancestors. This last sum bounds both loads at every gate. Also examine zero-margin directional penalties on the shared mixed example. Collect coefficients after Boolean multilinear reduction and exclude the constant and zeros when reporting magnitudes and their ratio. The zero-margin condition allows ties. Per-fiber summaries include every x, with pointwise raw data for the signed mixed control; all larger raw tables can be regenerated. No averages over random instances.

## Exact Gibbs summation

Use m=1,2,3, margin delta=1,2, and beta=ln(2),1,2 on the same Cartesian grid for (i) the ordinary directional shared chain with m gates and objective prod(x[0:m+1])*(2*x[m+1]-3*x[m+2]), and (ii) the IV.2 family Q=a*x+(delta+M*x)*sum(y), with M=0,1,2,4,8 and a=-log(R)/(2 beta), R=(1+exp(-beta*delta))^m. M=0 is the constant-fiber exact-marginal control. Sum every auxiliary state by log-sum-exp, then normalize over original inputs. Check both probability sums, repeat the calculation after shifting both energies by 19, and enforce TV <= b1 <= b2 from (26). Use absolute tolerance 1e-12 throughout these checks.

## Unfoldings and coordinate selection

Use every cut of the fixed graphs and orders in inputs/instances.json. Compare identity and the indicated single XOR on each graph. For the tree include root-bit and child-parent parity coordinates; decode by accumulation along the root path. Check all maps and inverses on every input, verify the explicit cubic (37) against a decoded cut, and record real multilinear degree. Compare on the tree's linear and edge monomial support with three independent signed integer coefficient draws from {-9,...,-1,1,...,9}, in mask order with the supplied seeds. Also compare the four crossing singleton coefficients in the tied product (x0+x1)(x2+x3) against independent draws on exactly the same support.

Unfold both values and the integer monomial coefficient tensor. Use exact real ranks by integer elimination. Validate (38) entrywise, support matching, crossing-term bound (39), quadratic bound (40) when applicable, and degree cap (41). Directly match full degree-permitted support at all 1<=k,l<=4, 0<=p<=k+l. Check (35) across identical cuts and orders using binary rank of both off-diagonal blocks of each coordinate map and inverse. For each graph compare the integer score and Gibbs distribution assigning mass proportional to 2^score (beta=ln(2), energy=-score); a global integer rescaling permits exact Gibbs ranks. Report SVD relative Frobenius-tail ranks at eta=0.1,0.01 separately for objective and Gibbs. Include the copy-lift energy, uniform marginal and normalized joint Gibbs with m=2,3,4, lambda=1, beta=ln(2), grouped and interleaved. Its exact joint rank at each cut is 2^s where s counts split pairs. Equality at a floating-point tail cutoff is included with a factor 1+1e-12 on the squared threshold. Integer equivalences, ranks and matching assertions require exact equality.

Coordinate screening considers only listed maps per fixed graph/order. Rank by maximum Gibbs 0.01-tail rank, then maximum exact objective rank, then degree, then maximum kappa, then map name. This is an explicit finite choice for the stated target. Store all candidate metrics so other targets can be considered. No global coordinate-selection or optimizer guarantee is assessed.
